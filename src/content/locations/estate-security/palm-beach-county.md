## A market of new arrivals

IRS migration data shows Palm Beach County gained $22.7 billion in net adjusted gross income from
domestic moves between 2019 and 2023, the most of any US county. The top origins were Nassau
County, Manhattan and Suffolk County, New York
([Miami Realtors, March 24, 2026](https://www.miamirealtors.com/2026/03/24/palm-beach-county-1-in-the-nation-in-net-inflow-of-income-from-domestic-migration-per-latest-irs-tax-data/)).
New owners often renovate before they move in, and that is when security gets designed.

## Record activity at the top of the market

The county recorded 141 single-family sales above $10 million in 2025, a record, up from 37 in
2019 ([Miami Realtors, February 4, 2026](https://www.miamirealtors.com/2026/02/04/south-florida-ultra-luxury-and-luxury-home-thresholds-rise-to-record-highs/)).
Redfin data cited by Fortune shows luxury home prices in the Palm Beach area rose 187% over the
past decade ([Fortune, March 14, 2026](https://www.fortune.com/2026/03/14/luxury-real-estate-markets-developer-wait-lists-subscriptions-billionaires)).

## Secure rooms that also make sense in Florida

A well-built safe room can serve more than one purpose. FEMA publishes design guidance for
residential safe rooms that protect against extreme wind
([FEMA, Safe Rooms](https://www.fema.gov/emergency-managers/risk-management/safe-rooms)).
Providers who explain both security and storm resilience, plainly and without alarm, give
households one clear place to start.
