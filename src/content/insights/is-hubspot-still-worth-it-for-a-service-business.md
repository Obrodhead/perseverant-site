## What does HubSpot still do well?

A lot. HubSpot remains one of the easiest CRMs to learn, and reviewers on Capterra still rate it
highly for ease of use (4.5 out of 5 across more than 4,000 reviews as of October 2026). Sales,
marketing and the website share one record of each customer, and the CMS includes hosting and
security so nobody on your team has to patch servers.

The business is still growing. HubSpot reported Q2 2026 revenue of $911.7 million, up 20% year
over year, across 306,446 customers ([HubSpot 8-K, August 5, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001404655/000119312526335148/hubs-ex99_1.htm)).
It is not a fading platform.

## Why do some users say it feels bloated?

The complaints are specific, and most of them are not about whether the CRM works. On HubSpot's
own community forum, users have asked since 2023 for a way to hide tools their team never uses,
and others have pushed back on frequent interface changes that break training documents. The
CMS draws a narrower critique: its HubL templating language and hosted stack make it harder to
move a site later, and the jump in cost is hard to justify for a simple brochure site.

In other words, "bloat" usually means too much surfaced to people who don't need it. That is a
setup and permissions problem as much as a product problem.

## How has HubSpot's pricing changed?

Three times in about two years:

- **March 2024:** seat-based pricing. Viewing is free, but editing records requires a paid seat.
- **June 2025:** one pool of HubSpot Credits for AI and data features. Credits reset monthly
  and don't roll over, and buying a pack defaults to an automatic upgrade for the rest of the
  contract ([HubSpot Knowledge Base](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/account-management/understand-hubspot-credits-and-billing)).
- **April 2026:** outcome pricing for two agents, $0.50 per resolved customer conversation and
  $1.00 per qualified prospecting lead ([HubSpot, April 13, 2026](https://www.hubspot.com/company-news/hubspots-customer-agent-and-prospecting-agent-now-you-pay-when-the-task-is-complete)).

Each change has a reasonable logic. Together they make costs harder to predict. HubSpot's CEO,
Yamini Rangan, acknowledged as much in August 2026, saying customers want "predictability in
what it costs" ([MarketBeat, August 8, 2026](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/hubspot-q2-earnings-call-highlights-2026-08-08/)).

Before you sign or renew, ask four questions:

1. How many people actually need to edit records?
2. At what contact count does our tier upgrade automatically?
3. What does the credit auto-upgrade do for the rest of our term?
4. How is a "resolved" conversation defined, in writing?

## Do HubSpot's Breeze AI agents actually work?

On narrow jobs with clean data, they can. HubSpot says its Customer Agent now resolves 72% of
support tickets without a human, and that more than 10,000 customers use it. Those are HubSpot's
own figures. In the same August 2026 discussion, Rangan named the biggest challenge plainly:
making sure agents that talk to customers produce solid outcomes ([CX Today, August 6, 2026](https://www.cxtoday.com/crm/hubspot-ai-agents-customer-agent-data-agent-cx-results/)).

Independent, hands-on evidence is still thin. The one point every source we reviewed agrees on
is that agent output tracks data quality. Duplicate contacts, inconsistent dropdown values and
loose permissions produce answers that look complete and are wrong.

This is not a HubSpot-only problem. In an October 2025 survey, Gartner found that 45% of
marketing technology leaders piloting AI agents said vendor-offered agents fell short of the
business results they were promised ([Gartner, October 29, 2025](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-10-29-gartner-survey-finds-45-percent-of-martech-leaders-say-existing-vendor-offered-ai-agents-fail-to-meet-their-expectations-of-promised-business-performance)).

## What matters more than which CRM you choose?

Speed and a human on the other end. Workato audited 114 B2B companies in 2026 and found an average
email reply time of nearly 12 hours. Only one replied within five minutes ([Workato, March 19, 2026](https://workato.com/the-connector/lead-response-time-study)).
That study comes from a company that sells routing automation, but the pattern matches years of
research. A 2011 Harvard Business Review study of more than 2,000 firms found that those who
contacted a lead within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify it as those who
waited longer.

People also still want a person. Gartner reported in August 2026 that 87% of customers say a
company using generative AI for service must offer access to a human agent ([Gartner, August 4, 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-08-04-gartner-survey-finds-87-percent-of-customers-say-companies-using-genai-for-customer-service-must-provide-access-to-a-human-agent0)).

For a law firm, a practice or a contractor, the expensive failure is rarely the wrong CRM. It's
an inquiry that waits overnight while a competitor calls back in ten minutes. That's why we
measure by booked appointments, not by clicks or contacts in a database.

## Does your website platform affect whether AI search finds you?

Less than how the site is built. Crawl data published by Vercel in December 2024 showed that
the crawlers behind ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity read the HTML a server sends and do not run
JavaScript ([Vercel, December 17, 2024](https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler)).
A HubSpot page with its content in the HTML can be read. A script-heavy page on any platform
may not be. We cover how to check this in
[Why Some Business Websites Are Invisible to ChatGPT and Claude](/insights/why-some-websites-are-invisible-to-ai-assistants/).

Beyond rendering, AI search rewards clarity. Your clients aren't just searching to find you.
They're searching to understand you. Pages that answer real questions plainly, with a named
author and current facts, give AI assistants something worth citing.

## So should you use HubSpot?

It depends on where you are:

- **Already on HubSpot:** stay for now. Clean your records, trim unused seats, and fix response
  times before adding agents. HubSpot now connects directly to Claude and ChatGPT, so your team
  can work with CRM data in the AI tools they already use.
- **No one in-house to run the system:** a supported all-in-one platform, HubSpot or another,
  is usually lower risk than a stack of tools nobody maintains.
- **You have a capable operator:** a lean CRM plus automation and AI connectors can do more for
  less. Practitioners are building skill in that connecting layer: HubSpot appeared in about
  half of the 1,000 GTM engineering job postings Bloomberry analyzed in 2025, alongside Clay,
  Zapier and n8n ([Bloomberry](https://bloomberry.com/blog/i-analyzed-1000-gtm-engineering-jobs-here-is-what-i-learned/)).
  They are working around the CRM, not abandoning it.

The tool is rarely the bottleneck. Fundamentals are: clean data, fast and human follow-up, and a
website that explains who you are well enough for both people and AI to understand it.
