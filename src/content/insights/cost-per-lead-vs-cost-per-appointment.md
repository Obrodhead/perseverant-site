## Why cost per lead misleads

Cost per lead treats every inquiry as equal. In businesses where clients research carefully before they commit,
such as a law firm, a cosmetic surgery practice or a roofing company, the difference between
a serious prospect and a tire-kicker is enormous. Optimizing for cheap leads often means
optimizing for the wrong people.

## A better chain of numbers

Track each step from first touch to revenue:

1. **Leads** by source.
2. **Booked appointments** (consultations, estimates, tours or discovery calls).
3. **Appointments held**, since no-shows cost real time.
4. **New clients** and the revenue they bring over time.

Divide spend by each step and the picture changes. A channel that looks expensive per lead
may be the cheapest per signed client.

## What improves the chain

Most gains come from the steps between the lead and the appointment: an instant response,
easy booking, reminders before the appointment and patient follow-up for people who are not
ready yet. These are cheaper to fix than buying more traffic, and they make every channel
perform better.

## Think in lifetime value

For many high-value businesses, the first sale is not the last. A patient returns for
related treatments, an employer sends repeat searches, a homeowner refers neighbors. Judging
marketing by lifetime value rather than first transaction supports investing in channels that
build trust, such as AI search visibility and educational events, even when they take longer
to pay off.
