## High tickets, short windows

A roof replacement or remodel is a large purchase, but the decision often moves fast once a
homeowner starts looking. The company that shows up first, looks credible and calls back
quickly gets the estimate.

## Where we focus

- **Service-area pages** for each town and service you want to win.
- **Google Business Profile and review strategy** that supports both maps and AI answers.
- **Paid campaigns** built around booked estimates.
- **Speed-to-lead automation** that texts and calls new leads within minutes.
