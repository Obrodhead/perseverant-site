## High tickets, short windows

A roof replacement or remodel is a large purchase, but the decision often moves fast once a homeowner
starts looking. The company that shows up first, looks credible and calls back quickly gets the estimate.

## Shared leads put you in a race on price

A lead sold to four contractors starts as a phone race and ends as a price fight. Inquiries that come
from your own site, profile and reputation start differently. The homeowner already chose to call you.
That's the business worth building, even if it grows slower at first.

## Reviews and profiles decide the shortlist

BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 68% of consumers require at least a 4-star rating, 89% expect owners to
respond to reviews and half are put off by templated replies ([BrightLocal, February 11, 2026](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/)).
Google says local results rest mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to
pay for a better local ranking ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)).
The work that wins the map is the same work that wins the homeowner: real reviews, real replies and clear
pages for each service and town.

## A steady market rewards the steady

Harvard's Joint Center for Housing Studies projects owner improvement and repair spending to grow from
$509 billion to $518 billion through late 2026, roughly 2% ([JCHS, April 15, 2026](https://www.jchs.harvard.edu/press-releases/remodeling-growth-set-downshift-late-2026)).
Slow growth means you win by earning share, not by riding a wave.

## Honest claims hold up

The FTC's 2024 rule bans fake reviews, including AI-written ones, and reviews bought with conditions
([Morgan Lewis, August 26, 2024](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2024/08/ftc-issues-final-rule-on-consumer-reviews-and-testimonials)).
Solar sellers must disclose total cost and financing terms and avoid overstated savings
([FTC, August 7, 2024](https://search.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2024/08/dont-waste-your-energy-solar-scam)).
We write claims you can stand behind. This is not legal advice.
