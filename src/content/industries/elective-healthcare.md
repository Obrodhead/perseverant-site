## Patients compare before they call

Someone considering implants or a cosmetic procedure reads, compares prices, watches videos and asks
detailed questions before booking. More of that now happens inside AI tools. BrightLocal's 2026 survey of
1,002 U.S. adults found the share using ChatGPT or other AI tools for local recommendations rose from 6%
to 45%, and 97% read reviews before choosing a local business ([BrightLocal, February 11, 2026](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/)).

So the question isn't only whether you rank. It's whether a patient who asks about cost, recovery and
candidacy gets a clear, honest answer that sounds like your practice.

## Demand is there, and so is competition

The American Society of Plastic Surgeons reported cosmetic surgical procedures rose 7% in 2025, with
facelifts up 13% and patients 66 and older up 24% ([ASPS, September 1, 2026](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/press-releases/lift-procedures-led-growth-as-demand-for-restoration-drove-plastic-surgery-in-2025-new-asps-report-reveals)).
In dentistry, the ADA Health Policy Institute found capacity opening up and wait times falling
([ADA News, October 1, 2025](https://adanews.ada.org/ada-news/2025/october/fiscal-squeeze-on-dental-practices-continues/)).
More open chairs means more practices competing for the same careful patients.

## Privacy is part of the plan

A federal court vacated part of HHS's online tracking guidance in June 2024, but tracking on patient
portals, scheduling pages and other pages that transmit health information is still covered, and vendors
handling it need a business associate agreement ([HHS](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/hipaa-online-tracking/index.html)).
Meta's 2025 restrictions on health advertisers can also block optimizing toward scheduling events
([Search Engine Land, March 11, 2025](https://searchengineland.com/meta-ads-restrictions-health-wellness-campaigns-453094)).
We keep health information out of ad platforms and analytics, and we count consults in your own systems.
This is not legal advice.

## Where we focus

- **Procedure pages** that answer cost, recovery and candidacy questions plainly, without promising results.
- **Reviews and profiles** that match what your site says, so AI answers do too.
- **Consult-focused campaigns** tracked to booked and attended visits, never built from patient lists.
- **Kind, patient nurture** for people who inquire but are months away from deciding.
