## The long research window

Someone considering implants or a cosmetic procedure reads, compares prices, watches videos
and asks AI assistants detailed questions before booking. Being present across that whole
window matters more than one perfect ad.

## Where we focus

- **Procedure pages** that answer cost, recovery and candidacy questions clearly.
- **Paid social campaigns** built around consultations, with tracking to booked and attended visits.
- **Nurture sequences** for patients who inquire but are months away from deciding.
