## Clients decide fast, and they decide on trust

Someone who needs a lawyer is often stressed and short on time. They search, read reviews, ask an AI
assistant, and contact more than one firm. The firm that explains things plainly and calls back first
often gets the conversation. Your marketing should make that easy, and your intake should keep up with it.

## Where we focus

- **Question-led content** for the searches people make before they call a lawyer, written
  to be quoted by AI assistants.
- **Local SEO** for every office, including attorney bio pages that show up for name searches.
- **Estate planning seminars and webinars** that let prospects meet an attorney before booking.
- **Intake automation** that responds to every form and call within minutes.
