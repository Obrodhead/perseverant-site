## Why law firms are a high-value niche

A single signed case or estate plan can justify months of marketing spend, so the economics
reward firms that capture demand reliably. The challenge is that potential clients compare
firms quickly and rarely wait.

## Where we focus

- **Question-led content** for the searches people make before they call a lawyer, written
  to be quoted by AI assistants.
- **Local SEO** for every office, including attorney bio pages that show up for name searches.
- **Estate planning seminars and webinars** that let prospects meet an attorney before booking.
- **Intake automation** that responds to every form and call within minutes.
