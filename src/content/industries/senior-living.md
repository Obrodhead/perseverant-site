## A considered, emotional decision

Families choosing care for a parent ask detailed questions about cost, safety and quality of
life. They want answers before they want a sales call. Firms that educate patiently earn
the tour.

## Where we focus

- **Answer pages** on cost, levels of care and how to choose, written for AI and Google.
- **Educational events and webinars** for families.
- **Tour-focused campaigns** with tracking from inquiry to move-in.
- **Long-term nurture** that respects the family's timeline.
