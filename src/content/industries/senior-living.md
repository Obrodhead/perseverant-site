## A considered, emotional decision

Families choosing care for a parent ask detailed questions about cost, safety and quality of life. They
want answers before they want a sales call. The community that educates patiently, and answers when the
family reaches out, earns the tour.

## Families often wait too long for a reply

In more than 250 mystery shops in 2023, Bild & Co. found 53% of web inquiries got no response within two
hours and 13% never got one. Inquiry-to-tour conversion averaged 22% ([Senior Housing News, February 12, 2024](https://seniorhousingnews.com/2024/02/12/senior-living-operators-level-up-sales-to-beat-alarming-trends-in-conversions/)).
Aline, a senior living CRM vendor, reported that web leads often need three to four touches to get a
response, and a move-in takes about 28 sales activities ([Aline, March 4, 2025](https://alineops.com/blog/takeaways-2024-aline-benchmark-report/)).
Patience is the strategy. A quick first reply, then steady, kind follow-up on the family's timeline.

## Referral sites can cost more than they bring

Senior Housing News reported one operator that received 465 referral-site leads in 2024, saw 12 move-ins
and cancelled the contracts ([Senior Housing News](https://seniorhousingnews.com/?p=49827)). Families who
find you directly, through your own pages and reviews, arrive knowing who you are.

## Ad rules shape who you can reach

Senior housing ads can fall under Meta's housing Special Ad Category, which fixes age targeting at 18–65+
and restricts ZIP targeting ([Meta Marketing API documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/special-ad-category)).
The message has to do the targeting, so we write to the adult child. Claims that a community is 55+ depend
on the Housing for Older Persons Act exemption ([NAHB, November 30, 2021](https://www.nahb.org/blog/2021/11/Advertising-Guidelines-for-Age-Restricted-Communities-Under-the-FHA)).
This is not legal advice.

## For home care, staffing comes first

Activated Insights reported caregiver turnover of 79.2%, and 63.3% of home care providers turned down
cases in 2023 because they couldn't staff them ([Home Health Care News, August 2024](https://homehealthcarenews.com/2024/08/caregiver-turnover-referral-rejections-continue-to-increase-in-home-care/)).
We plan client marketing around the hours you can actually fill.

## Where we focus

- **Answer pages** on cost, levels of care and how to choose, written for AI assistants and Google.
- **Educational events and webinars** for families who aren't ready to tour yet.
- **Tour-focused campaigns** tracked from inquiry to move-in.
- **Long-term nurture** that respects the family's timeline.
