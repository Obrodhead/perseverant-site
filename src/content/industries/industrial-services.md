## The decision is repair or replace

When a tank, pipe or duct starts failing, the plant weighs replacement against repair. That choice
gets researched: which standards apply, how long the repair will last, and whether it can be done
while the line keeps running. The contractor that answers those questions clearly is the one
engineers call.

## Downtime is the real cost

Siemens reported in 2024 that a large plant still loses an average of 27 hours a month to
unplanned downtime, down from 39 hours in 2019
([Siemens, The True Cost of Downtime 2024](https://assets.new.siemens.com/siemens/assets/api/uuid:1b43afb5-2d07-47f7-9eb7-893fe7d0bc59/TCOD-2024_original.pdf)).
Repair work that avoids a shutdown is worth a lot to a plant, and buyers want proof before they let
a crew on site.

## Several people decide

A maintenance manager may find you first. A reliability engineer checks the method. Procurement asks
about safety records and insurance. We build pages and follow-up for each of them, so a good inquiry
doesn't stall between desks.
