## Why estate security is a high-value niche

A single engagement can include an assessment, design work, a safe room and ongoing protection,
and the relationship often lasts as long as the family owns the home. Demand has grown. ASIS
International reported in April 2025 that the share of S&P 500 companies providing a security
benefit to a named executive rose to 31.3% in fiscal 2024, up from 24.5% the year before
([ASIS, April 30, 2025](https://www.asisonline.org/security-management-magazine/latest-news/today-in-security/2025/april/enterprise-ep-spending/)).
Many of the boutique firms that do this work well are still found almost entirely by word of mouth.

## Buyers search to understand, not just to find

Households rarely know what to ask for. They start with questions: what an assessment covers,
what a safe room involves, how to protect a home they live in only part of the year. We write
clear answers to those questions, so your firm becomes the one that explained it first, in
Google and in AI assistants.

## Referral partners do the introducing

Security is often specified early, before a new owner moves in. We help you earn introductions
from the architects, builders, estate managers and luxury agents who are already involved, through
short continuing-education seminars, partner resources and quick follow-up.

## Fast response, human tone

When a household or its estate manager reaches out, timing matters. We set up routing and
follow-up so a real person from your team replies quickly and discreetly, and the inquiry
becomes a booked assessment.
