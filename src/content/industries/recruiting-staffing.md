## Selling to hiring managers

Employers choose recruiting partners based on specialization and proof they can fill hard
roles. Content and campaigns should show deep knowledge of the industries and positions you
serve.

## Where we focus

- **Industry and role pages** that show your specialization to hiring managers and AI assistants.
- **B2B paid social campaigns** aimed at decision-makers.
- **Automated outreach and nurture** for employers between hiring cycles.
