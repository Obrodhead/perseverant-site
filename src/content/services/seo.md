## What we focus on

Most service business websites lose prospects to three problems: pages search engines
struggle to read, a thin local presence, and generic content that answers nobody's real
question. We fix them in that order, because content cannot rank on a site Google cannot crawl.

## Search Essence Optimization

We think about SEO a little differently. Your clients aren't just searching to find you. They're
searching to understand you: what you do best, who you do it for and whether you're the right fit.
Google and AI assistants are trying to answer the same questions on their behalf.

So before we chase a ranking, we find the essence of your business and say it plainly, the same way,
on every page, profile and listing. When people and search systems reach the same understanding of
you, rankings tend to follow, and the prospects who call already know why they're calling. Our job is
to meet them in the middle.

## Built to work with AI search

Every SEO page we build also follows the structure AI assistants prefer: a direct answer
first, clear headings, and schema. The same work earns rankings and citations.
