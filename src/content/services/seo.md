## What we focus on

Most service business websites lose prospects to three problems: pages search engines
struggle to read, a thin local presence, and generic content that answers nobody's real
question. We fix them in that order, because content cannot rank on a site Google cannot crawl.

## Built to work with AI search

Every SEO page we build also follows the structure AI assistants prefer: a direct answer
first, clear headings, and schema. The same work earns rankings and citations.
