## Where event campaigns pay off

Events work best when the decision is large and the buyer wants to learn first: estate
planning workshops, family information sessions about senior care, or a surgeon's patient
seminar. They turn a cold prospect into someone who has already met you.

## Where most event campaigns break

Filling registrations is the easy part. The money is lost between registration and the
event, and again between the event and the first appointment. We build the reminder and
follow-up steps with as much care as the ads.
