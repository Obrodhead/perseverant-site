## Why this matters now

Prospects increasingly start with a question typed into an AI assistant instead of a list
of links. The firms named in those answers get the first conversation. The firms that are
missing are invisible at the exact moment a prospect is deciding who to call.

## How we approach it

We start with the questions, not keywords. For a firm that serves business owners, that
might be "how should I plan for selling my company" rather than "financial advisor near me."
Each question gets a page with a direct answer up top, the reasoning below it, and schema
that tells machines exactly who wrote it and what it covers.

Then we work on the signals outside your site: consistent firm and advisor profiles,
directory listings and mentions on publications your prospects read.
