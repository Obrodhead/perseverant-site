## Why this matters now

More buyers start with a question typed into an AI assistant instead of a list of links.
The businesses named in those answers get the first conversation. The ones missing are
invisible at the moment a prospect decides who to call.

## How we approach it

We start with questions, not keywords. For a roofing company that might be "how do I know if
my roof needs replacing after a storm" rather than "roofer near me." Each question gets a page
with a direct answer up top, the reasoning below it, and schema that tells machines exactly
who wrote it and what it covers.

Then we work on signals outside your site: consistent business profiles, directory listings,
reviews and mentions on publications your customers read.
