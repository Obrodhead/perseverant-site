## Grassroots first, then sharpen

Ads amplify whatever they point to. When your site, reviews and profiles already explain who you are,
a good ad brings people to a business they can understand and trust quickly. When they don't, ads mostly
buy attention that leaks away. So we look at what a prospect will find after the click before we spend
on the click.

## One offer, one clear reason to raise a hand

Most people scrolling a feed aren't looking for you yet. A consultation, an estimate, an educational
event or a guide to a question they already have gives them a reason to step forward. We build each
campaign around one offer, write the first line the way a person would say it, and keep testing creative
and audiences from there.

## Meta for households, LinkedIn for roles

Meta reaches homeowners, patients and families. LinkedIn lets you target by job title, company, industry
and seniority (LinkedIn Marketing Solutions, accessed October 20, 2026), so it suits B2B buyers such as
hiring managers and plant engineers. Housing, employment and credit ads fall under Meta's Special Ad
Categories, which fix age targeting at 18–65+, restrict gender and ZIP targeting and remove lookalike
audiences (Meta Marketing API documentation). We plan around those rules from the start.

## The click is the start, not the result

A lead who waits hours for a reply often books with someone else. Every campaign ships with follow-up
that answers fast, hands off to a person and tracks each lead to the booked appointment.
