# What owners search for, by niche (October 2026)

Research behind the six service-page guides. These are the questions business **owners** ask (not their customers), gathered from industry publications, vendor reports and practitioner forums in October 2026. No keyword-volume tool was used, so treat this as intent research, not search volume. Verified facts with sources live in `guide-spec.md`.

## Law firms (PI, estate planning, family, mass torts, criminal)
- AI: "How do I get my firm recommended by ChatGPT?" "Does AI search still send leads, can I track it?"
- SEO: "Rank in the map pack for 'car accident lawyer near me'", "get more Google reviews ethically", "LSA vs SEO", "is my SEO agency worth it?"
- Paid social: "Do Facebook ads work for PI/family law?" "Cost per signed case?" "Why are Facebook leads junk?" Mass torts: pixel/event optimization stopped working.
- Seminars: "Do estate planning seminars still work?" "Fill a seminar with Facebook ads", "seminar-to-client conversion".
- Automation: "We miss after-hours calls", "Lawmatics vs Clio Grow", "AI chatbot for intake?"
- Tools: "Connect CallRail, CRM and case management", "cost per signed case by source".
- Constraints: ABA 7.1–7.3, no specialist claims without certification, Florida/Texas filing, Florida Opinion 24-1 on AI chatbots, Texas HB 2733 (electronic solicitation).

## Elective healthcare (implants, ortho, plastics, med spa, LASIK)
- AI: "Why does ChatGPT recommend the practice across town?" "What does AI say about our prices?"
- SEO: "Not in the map pack for 'dental implants [city]'", "separate pages per procedure/location?"
- Paid social: "How much should a full-arch lead cost?" "Why did Meta leads get worse in 2025?" "Can we still retarget?"
- Seminars: "Do implant seminars still work?" "Half the RSVPs don't show."
- Automation: "How fast to call back?" "Cut consult no-shows", "what to text after a consult that went quiet".
- Tools: "Ad leads into scheduling software", "send conversions to Meta without HIPAA risk", "financing/cost estimator".
- Constraints: HHS tracking guidance (partly vacated June 2024), Meta 2025 health restrictions, FTC reviews rule, state medical/dental board ad rules.

## Home improvement (roofing, remodeling, HVAC, solar, windows)
- AI: "Will ChatGPT recommend my roofing company?" "Why does AI Overview list my competitor?"
- SEO: "Rank for 'roofer near me'", "GBP calls dropped", "service-area business without storefront", "SEO or just buy leads?" "Leads without Angi?" "Shared vs exclusive leads", "Are LSAs worth it now?"
- Paid social: "Do Meta ads work for roofers/solar/windows?" "Fake numbers", "daily spend?"
- Events: "Are home shows worth the booth fee?" "Follow up home show leads", "homeowner workshops".
- Automation: "Call back while crews are on roofs", "estimate no-shows", "JobNimbus vs ServiceTitan vs AccuLynx", "is auto-texting legal?"
- Tools: "Angi, LSA and Facebook leads into my CRM automatically", "instant estimate tool".
- Constraints: FTC reviews rule, TCPA consent and revocation, FTC solar guidance.

## Senior living and home care
- AI: "Why doesn't ChatGPT mention us for 'best memory care near [city]'?" "Is AI showing old pricing?"
- SEO: "Rank for 'assisted living near me' without paying A Place for Mom", "one site/profile per community?"
- Paid social: "Reach adult children without age targeting", "junk Facebook leads".
- Events: "Lunch-and-learns that bring move-ins", "family education night topics", "get discharge planners to come".
- Automation: "How fast to call web inquiries?" "Lead-to-tour conversion", "Enquire vs Aline vs WelcomeHome", "nurture families not ready for 6–12 months".
- Tools: "Which source produced move-ins?" "Pricing/care-level estimator".
- Constraints: Meta housing Special Ad Category, HOPA for 55+ claims, TCPA, HIPAA for covered home health.

## Recruiting and staffing (employer side)
- AI: "Why does ChatGPT recommend my competitor for 'best healthcare staffing agency in [city]'?"
- SEO: "We rank for job seekers, not employers", "a GBP per branch?"
- Paid social: "Do LinkedIn ads work for staffing or just expensive?" "Target HR directors and plant managers", "Meta for clients or only candidates?"
- Webinars: "Salary-guide or hiring-outlook webinar that brings in hiring managers", "do salary reports turn into job orders?"
- Automation: "Automate BD follow-up in Bullhorn without spamming", "why cold emails land in spam since 2024".
- Tools: "Connect ATS to forms, call tracking and ads", "rate/salary calculator", "which channel produced placements".
- Constraints: CAN-SPAM applies to B2B, Gmail/Yahoo/Outlook sender rules, LinkedIn automation terms, employment Special Ad Category for job ads.

## Estate security
Covered from the existing industry page and ASIS data; owner questions center on discreet visibility, referral paths with architects and estate managers, and explaining the assessment process before a call.

## Gaps
No credible public benchmarks were found for cost per signed case, seminar-to-client conversion, full-arch cost per lead, or home care lead response, so the guides use labeled example math instead.
